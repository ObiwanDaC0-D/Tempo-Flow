Advanced H10 capabilities and limitations

Polar H10 also supports 130 Hz ECG, 3-axis accelerometer streams at supported rates, and onboard HR recording. These use Polar-specific PMD/PFTP protocols, not the standard Bluetooth Heart Rate Service. They are not implemented in this browser build and must not be mistaken for collected data. Raw ECG/accelerometer access requires a separately validated Polar protocol implementation or native Polar SDK bridge. H10 does not have optical PPG, gyroscope, GPS, or skin temperature sensing.
